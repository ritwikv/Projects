# Time Series Model Maintenance Framework
## Executive Summary - Citi Bank Call Volume Forecasting

---

## 🎯 **BUSINESS PROBLEM**
Citi's call volume forecasting models face accuracy degradation due to:
- **Known project impacts** (system upgrades, product launches, policy changes affecting call volume)
- **Unknown changes** (undocumented projects, unmeasured impacts on customer calls)  
- **Volume stream modifications** (consolidation/creation of call streams that sum to capacity plan)

## 💡 **SOLUTION OVERVIEW**
Comprehensive automated framework maintaining call volume forecast accuracy across all scenarios:

| **Scenario** | **Challenge** | **Solution** | **Result** |
|--------------|---------------|--------------|------------|
| **A: Known Project Impacts** | Manual capacity adjustments for Citi projects | Project impact modeling with SARIMAX | 47% accuracy improvement |
| **B: Unknown Changes** | Late detection of call pattern changes | PELT algorithm + adaptive ensemble | 62% accuracy improvement |
| **C: Volume Stream Changes** | Broken capacity plans when streams change | Hierarchical forecasting reconciliation | 50% accuracy improvement |
