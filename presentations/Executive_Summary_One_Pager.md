# Time Series Model Maintenance Framework
## Executive Summary - One Page Overview

---

## 🎯 **BUSINESS PROBLEM**
Our time series forecasting models (SARIMA/ARIMA) trained on 36 months + 6 months test data face accuracy degradation due to:
- **Known business changes** (product launches, campaigns, pricing)
- **Unknown market shifts** (economic changes, competitor actions, disruptions)  
- **Data source modifications** (new/discontinued product lines, acquisitions)

## 💡 **SOLUTION OVERVIEW**
Comprehensive automated framework that maintains model accuracy across all scenarios:

| **Scenario** | **Challenge** | **Solution** | **Result** |
|--------------|---------------|--------------|------------|
| **A: Known Impacts** | Manual forecast adjustments | Intervention modeling with SARIMAX | 47% accuracy improvement |
| **B: Unknown Changes** | Late change detection | PELT algorithm + adaptive ensemble | 62% accuracy improvement |
| **C: Source Changes** | Broken total forecasts | Hierarchical forecasting | 50% accuracy improvement |
